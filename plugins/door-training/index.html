Door Anatomy & Terminology

Tap each part on the door above as you read. Mark each step done to track progress.
ℹ️
This lesson models a typical wide-body Type A plug door (e.g. 777, A350 main cabin door). Always defer to your aircraft's CCOM / FCOM for the exact procedure.
1
Viewing port (window)
The viewing port lets you check outside conditions before operating the door: jet bridge / stairs in position, no fire or smoke, no obstructions, no people in the slide path.
2
Door operating handle
The primary lever used to unlock and open the door. Travel is typically up/around through 180°. Power-assist (EPA — Emergency Power Assist) accelerates opening when armed.
3
Mode select lever
Selects ARMED (automatic — slide deploys on opening) or DISARMED (manual — slide stowed). Configured per the captain's command and confirmed by cross-check.
4
Indicator window
Visual confirmation of door state. SAFE (green) = closed, locked & latched. A red / striped indication = unlocked or unsafe. Confirm before takeoff and landing.
5
Slide / raft bustle
Housing at the lower door containing the evacuation slide / slide-raft. When ARMED, the slide is attached to the cabin floor via the girt bar; opening the door deploys it.
6
Girt bar
The red bar that anchors the slide to the cabin floor when armed. Visible only when ARMED. If you see it on a "doors disarmed" command — stop and verify.
7
Gust lock
Holds the door fully open against wind so it can't slam back. Engage on every normal open; release deliberately when closing.

Normal Operation

Arm, disarm, open and close. Practise each on the door above.
⚠️
Always operate doors only on command from the flight deck, and confirm via cross-check with the opposite-side crew.
1
Arm slide (before pushback)
On the captain's command "Cabin Crew — arm doors and cross-check":
  • Move the Mode Select lever to ARMED.
  • Confirm girt bar is visible and attached to the floor brackets.
  • Cross-check the opposite door and reply by interphone.
2
Disarm slide (after arrival)
On the captain's command "Cabin Crew — disarm doors and cross-check":
  • Move Mode Select to DISARMED.
  • Confirm girt bar is stowed (no red bar visible).
  • Cross-check and report.
3
Open door — normal (disarmed)
Only when DISARMED:
  • Look through the viewing port — confirm bridge / stairs in position, area clear.
  • Operate the handle through its full travel.
  • Push the door fully open until the gust lock engages.
4
Close door
Confirm jet bridge is clear, release the gust lock, swing the door closed firmly, then move the handle through its full closing travel until the indicator reads SAFE / green.
⛔
Never open a door in ARMED mode in normal operation. Doing so will deploy the slide — significant cost, possible injury, and aircraft delay.

Safety Checks & Cross-checks

When and what to check, every flight.
1
Pre-flight check
Before boarding:
  • Door indicator SAFE / green.
  • Mode Select DISARMED.
  • No visible girt bar; bustle area clear of obstructions.
  • Viewing port unobstructed and clean.
  • Slide pressure gauge in green band (if visible).
2
Arming cross-check
On "Arm doors and cross-check":
  • Set Mode Select to ARMED.
  • Visually confirm girt bar attached to floor.
  • Make eye contact with the opposite-side crew; visually confirm their lever is ARMED.
  • Report on interphone: "Door [position] armed and cross-checked."
3
Disarming cross-check
On "Disarm doors and cross-check", mirror the arming procedure in reverse: lever to DISARMED, girt bar stowed, cross-check opposite, report.
4
Before opening (after landing)
Even after a disarm cross-check, before pulling the handle:
  • Mode Select confirmed DISARMED by you, again.
  • Bridge / stairs confirmed in position via the viewing port.
  • Ground crew signal received.
Treat the disarm cross-check and the open-the-door check as two separate gates.
💡
Memory aid: "Mode → Indicator → Outside → Handle" — a quick four-point check every time, every door.

Emergency Operation

Evacuation: when and how to deploy.
⛔
Emergency evacuation is initiated only on the captain's command, or by senior cabin crew if the cabin becomes untenable (fire, smoke, structural damage) and the flight deck is unresponsive.
1
Assess outside conditions
Before opening any door in evacuation:
  • Look through the viewing port. Check for fire, smoke, debris, fuel, water level, obstacles.
  • If unsafe — redirect passengers to a usable exit. Block the unusable door and shout "Door blocked! Go to next exit!"
2
Confirm ARMED, then open
With outside safe:
  • Confirm Mode Select is ARMED — if not, arm it before pulling the handle.
  • Operate the handle through full travel. EPA accelerates the door.
  • Stand clear of the door arc.
3
Verify slide deployed & inflated
Slide must be:
  • Fully inflated and stable.
  • Reaching the ground at the correct angle.
  • Clear of fire / obstructions.
If not deployed: pull the manual inflation handle. If still not deployed — block the exit, redirect.
4
Command evacuation
Shouted commands, clear and continuous:
  • "Release seatbelts! Leave everything! Come this way!"
  • At the door: "Jump and slide! Arms forward! Jump!"
  • Direct flow — two passengers at a time on a dual-lane slide.
Last off: grab the emergency equipment (megaphone, ELT, first aid) if safe and time permits, then evacuate.
5
Ditching: door & slide-raft
Over water, the slide also serves as a raft after detachment:
  • Open door normally (armed) — slide-raft inflates.
  • Once survivors are on board, cut the mooring line with the attached hook knife.
  • Deploy sea anchor and locate survival kit.
